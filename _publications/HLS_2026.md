This case study offers a comprehensive, engaging resource that blends advanced data analytics and critical thinking in a cost accounting setting. Students start with data cleaning and descriptive statistics, progressing to visualizations and multivariate regression. The case also involves advanced tasks like building a random forest model, analyzing its performance, and refining predictive models. Students apply predictive models to a new dataset, and an open-ended question asks them to use results to inform a meaningful business decision. Finally, students write a memo to a client, improving both their analytical and communication skills. The case is designed to be implemented as an end-of-semester comprehensive assessment of student skills in a graduate-level accounting data analytics course. Case requirements are written to be adaptable to require other machine learning models. Case solutions are provided in R and Python.
